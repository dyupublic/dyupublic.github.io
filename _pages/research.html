Research interest: I am particularly interested in leveraging text data to better understand individual and community health. I apply natural language processing and machine learning on large-scale health data with a focus on modeling problems and proposing novel algorithms for real-world applications including but not limited to social media and medical data.

Please see here for my published work.

Projects

• Neighborhood-Level Influenza Forecasting in Boston Using Graph Neural Networks: This project aims to investigate how macro-level, demographic, temporal, and geospatial features can be integrated using GNNs to forecast influenza activity at the neighborhood level in a densely populated and highly connected city such as Boston.
• Online Mental Health Discussions: This project aims to investigate mental health discourse in online communities with a focus on identifying common themes, language use, and cultural context.
